Privacy Policy

Harmonic Affirmations is an iOS app by C.B.Mack. It lets you record your own spoken affirmations and play them with looping binaural beats. This policy describes what the app does with information.

Recordings stay on your device

When you record an affirmation, the audio file is saved on your phone. We do not upload recordings to our servers. We do not have a user account system. Clearing the app or using the in-app clear action removes the library metadata from the app; you can also delete the app to remove local files.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you start a recording. Permission is requested by iOS. Audio is not shared with other apps by us.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, iOS schedules local notifications on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server.

Purchases

Harmonic Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through the App Store. Apple processes payment. We use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. We do not receive your card number. Apple’s privacy policy applies to App Store billing.

Analytics and advertising

The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs beyond what Apple and RevenueCat require to validate a purchase.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Do not use it to collect information from a child.

Not medical advice

Binaural beats and affirmations are wellness tools. They are not a medical device or treatment. This is not medical, mental-health, or sleep advice.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the Harmonic Affirmations App Store listing to contact the developer.
