How to record affirmations in your own voice

Still looping affirmations in someone else’s voice? Write one line, record it yourself, and play it over a Theta binaural beat. Three steps, all on the free tier of Harmonic Affirmations.

Three steps

Write it, record it, play it over Theta

  1. Write your line

    One sentence, first person, in your own words. Or start from one of the 2 scripts that come free in the app.

    “I am enough.”

  2. Record it in your own voice

    Open Harmonic Affirmations and record the line with your phone’s mic. The free tier includes 1 recording. The file is saved on your device.

  3. Play it over Theta

    Pick the Theta beat, which is included free, and play your recording over it. That’s the whole practice: your line, your voice, over the beat.
